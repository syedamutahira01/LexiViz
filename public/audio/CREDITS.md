# Audio credits

- **Background music:** generated live in the browser with the Web Audio API (src/audio/ambient.ts, src/audio/music.ts). Original chord progression, no samples, no vocals. No third-party license applies.
- **Custom music (optional):** drop `public/audio/music/loop.mp3` here and it replaces the generated loop. Record its title, author and license below.
- **Narration voice:** AI-generated. Pre-generated files come from the provider named in `public/audio/manifest.json`; otherwise your browser's built-in speech synthesis speaks. No real person's voice is cloned.
- **Translations:** Urdu and Roman Urdu text is AI-translated and marked "ai-translated" until a native speaker reviews it.
